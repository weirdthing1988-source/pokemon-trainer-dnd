Rules & Settings

Prototype Formulae

Stat Rank: round(Base Stat ÷ 17), with .5 rounding up.

Stat Modifier: floor((Rank − 5) ÷ 2).

Max HP: Base HP + (Level − 1) × (ceil(Base HP ÷ 2) + 1).

Accuracy Score: round(8 + Move Accuracy ÷ 20); Accuracy Modifier = Score − 10.

Attack Roll: d20 + Trainer PB + Offensive Stat Modifier + Accuracy Modifier.

Base Damage Dice: ceil(Offensive Rank ÷ 2). Move sets the die size.

Sure Hit: bypasses the To Hit roll.
