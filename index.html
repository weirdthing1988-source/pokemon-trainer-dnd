Rules & Settings

Prototype Formulae

Stat Rank: round(Base Stat ÷ 17), with .5 rounding up.

Stat Modifier: floor((Rank − 5) ÷ 2).

Level 1 HP: raw Base HP.

HP Growth: round(Base HP ÷ 10) + 1 for each level after Level 1.

Accuracy Score: round(8 + Move Accuracy ÷ 20); Accuracy Modifier = Score − 10.

Attack Roll: d20 + eligible Trainer PB + Offensive Stat Modifier + Accuracy Modifier.

Base Damage Dice: ceil(Offensive Rank ÷ 2). PokéAPI move power sets the provisional die size.

Trainer Proficiency: PB and PB-based milestone dice apply only to Pokémon the Trainer is proficient commanding.

Sure Hit: bypasses the To Hit roll.
